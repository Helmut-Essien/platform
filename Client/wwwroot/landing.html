How licensing and payments work

No surprises: you always know what you pay, when, and for what.

  1. Get your licence

    Choose the software you need. We issue a licence for your business with a clear subscription period.

  2. Receive your invoice

    Your invoice comes by email in Ghana cedis (GHS), with a PDF copy and a secure pay link.

    Before your licence expires you get a renewal invoice. Pay it and your licence extends automatically.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does software licensing work?

You pay a subscription fee for each software licence. The licence stays active for its subscription period and is renewed when the renewal invoice is paid.

What currency are invoices in?

All invoices are issued in Ghana cedis (GHS).

How can I pay?

Pay by Mobile Money or by card through Paystack. Each invoice email includes a secure pay link, so you do not need an account to pay.

What happens when my licence is due for renewal?

A renewal invoice is emailed before your licence expires. Once it is paid, your licence is renewed automatically for the next period.

Do you provide support?

Yes. Customers with an active licence can contact us for support.

Can you build software for my business?

Yes. Get in touch and tell us what your business needs.
